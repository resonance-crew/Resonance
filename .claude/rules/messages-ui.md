# Messages: stickers, the composer, the thread

## Stickers and the composer (round 22)

**Stickers**: five packs, one per paper puppet as its protagonist (the owner's choice — people pick a set by its character; a new character opens a new pack, a new idea joins that character's), no words on the art.
Ids and names (zh-TW / en) in `src/lib/stickers/catalog.ts` — stored in messages for good, never renamed (`isSticker`, `stickerName`, `stickerFallbackText`, `stickerPath`); the art's numbers in the generated `catalog.generated.ts` (with each sticker's drawn area, round 24: `box` over every frame, `poseBox` its key frame — [left, top, right, bottom] fractions, measured by `scripts/stickers/catalog.ts`, in `catalog.json` too — so the thread lays a sticker out by its drawing, the empty paper above and below taking no room and nothing clipped, and a quote centres the thumb's drawing at 92 % of its square: the owner saw 點點 stand low with empty paper over it); the files in `public/stickers/<pack>/` (animated WebP 432², 12 fps, two plays baked in, ≤ 120 KB target, 200 KB refused; `.thumb.webp` 256² static; `icon.webp` the pack's tab) and `catalog.json`, served `immutable` (catalog an hour) and bundled by both apps (iOS a folder reference to `public/stickers`, Android the `syncStickers` task); a sticker a build lacks is fetched from the site, else its fallback words show.
Source of truth `designs/stickers` (README: the kit — `defineSticker` needs `hero`, the moment that reads as the sticker's meaning; the file starts and ends there and its thumb is that frame —, each pack's choreographies and its `_local.ts` props; `review/index.html` plays them all).
The picker sends on one tap (round 23: no preview; the same sticker again within 600 ms is ignored; 🕘 recents are held as they were while the picker is open) and breathes: a cell ≥ 60 % in view plays its file once, rests 1.5–3 s on its thumb (the file starts and ends on it, so the swap is seamless), plays again — the picker's own limiter, never the thread's: 6 at once on a web phone, 8 on the apps' phones, 12 on a tablet or in a popover, first come, first served (a cell waits from when it comes into view or its rest ends; reading order only among cells that start waiting together, so none is starved on a grown panel), 80 ms apart, timed from when its file shows (a file that can't be shown holds its turn a play's length, so a failing site isn't asked every 80 ms), none while the grid scrolls (iOS: a finger resting on it isn't scrolling — `ScrollPhase.moves`, as for the bands), all still under reduced motion (the web: Save-Data too) and under nothing else — Low Power Mode and battery saver change nothing, neither the limit nor the breathing (the owner, round 25: 「省电模式应该没有影响这么大」; their phone in battery saver showed a picker that never moved) (web `lib/chat/stickerBreath`, iOS `StickerBreathing`, Android `PickerBreath` in `StickerBreath.kt`, which reads the still state at every tick, so a setting changed with the picker open takes at the next; a playing cell there carries the `StickerMoving` semantics flag and, in debug builds, the flow tag `sticker-moving` the `sticker-breath` flow waits for); the grid 8 from the edges, 4 between cells, 2 inside each (4 columns on a phone, a wider panel more — the web's 5 from 520, 6 from 680; the apps' tablets 6), the desktop and Mac popover 400 × 460 in 4 columns, hanging from the capsule's top and closing on a send.
Clients play a sticker's two plays on first sight in a thread (at most three at once, off-screen stopped), rest on the thumb, replay on a tap, and show the thumb alone under reduced motion — never the thumb under playing frames.

**The composer**: `[＋][ field … ☺ ][Send]`. ☺ shows only while the field holds no words (a sticker is sent alone).
Phones and iPad: ☺ and ＋ swap the keyboard for a panel in its place at the height the keyboard covers of the window (its height remembered per orientation, less any gap between the window's foot and the screen's — iPadOS's windowed mode; round 24: the swap moves nothing), the foot's own paper, no card or divider — the sticker panel (pack bar of the five heads plus 🕘 recents, one continuous grid with pack headings, fades at its edges, a tap sends; ↑/↓ grows it while there is nothing to send) and the attach panel (a wavy grip, 圖片｜卡片 floating with nothing behind it; iOS the inline system PhotosPicker — on its own paper: while 圖片 shows the whole panel (grip band, the panes' gutter, down to the window's foot) and the attach popover's sheet are `systemBackground`, one colour, 卡片 the cream (round 24) —, Android its own MediaStore gallery — `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / partial access, the system picker when refused; Play's Photo and Video Permissions declaration must be on file).
Desktop web and Mac — and an iPad where no on-screen keyboard would take a panel's place (a hardware keyboard, a floating or split one, decided at the tap; round 24: switching stickers ↔ text moves nothing) —: ☺ opens a PaperSheet popover (the apps' ＋ its attach sheet — on the Mac nothing of ours is drawn over its photo picker, the pen line under it, and the sheet is laid where it hangs, never moved or scaled there: a view or a transform over that out-of-process picker leaves it taking no click, round 24); the web's ＋ keeps its small menu (圖片 opens a multi-file input; drop and paste too).
What rides with a message rides inside the capsule, above the text line (round 23, the owner's — Telegram's way: no rows over it, no fade for them): the reply, a note, a card, the chosen pictures, the apps' error line, in that order, each in three columns — its mark (≤ 32: the reply's arrow — the `reply` glyph at 20, in the accent, round 24, where a wavy rule stood —, the note's or card's tile — Android's tile 36 in its 48 column, round 24: the owner found its card picture smaller than iOS's) in the ＋'s own column (web and iOS 44, Android 48), its words where the field's text starts (a reply's media — a sticker, a picture, a quoted card's cover — a 36 thumbnail before them), its 44 ✕ centred over Send's 52 column; rows 44 (a two-line row's lines 1.2 apart with nothing between, round 24; a large text size grows a row, never clips it), 6 under the pen line, nothing between them or before the text line, so one reply makes the capsule 98 (the foot was 124); the pictures a strip 8 + 56 + 8 inside the outline, each thumbnail with its own ✕ (no numbers, no count; named 移除第 n 張圖片), its ends fading, a vertical wheel scrolling it sideways; up to 10; one Send sends them as an album, the words, a note and a reply on picture 0; a card and pictures never ride together (asked first).
From the second line, and whenever anything rides, Send is a 52 × 48 block on the last row (its top-left corner round); from the third a square ghost ⤢ sits at the top of the text area in Send's column, under the rows; past five lines the text scrolls to the pen line, fading at the edge where more lies beyond; ⤢ grows the same text view (option C) to just under the other person's last message, or under the header when that one is tall, ⤡ in the same place — the whole capsule sized, the rows counted (iOS `ComposerGrowth`), the tablets' 60 % cap on the capsule.
Tapping the reply's words jumps to the quoted message; a press anywhere else in the capsule focuses the field and nothing in it takes the keyboard away (web `BareIconButton keepFocus`); Escape cancels the reply when no panel, popover or menu is open; the rows are one group for a screen reader (「隨訊息一起送出」) and starting a reply says so once; a grown sticker panel and the thread's clearance are measured again whenever the capsule's height changes (a sticker sent as a reply drops it 98 → 48).
On a short window (`(pointer: coarse) and (max-height: 500px)`, compact height) each row is one line of 36 (label · detail), thumbnails 44, the text three lines; on the Mac the rows' type is 13/14.
The outline is steady (web `wobRect` `steady` through `segmentCapsuleShape(…, { steady: true })`, the apps' twins): at one line it is the old shape point for point, taller only the sides and the top are drawn anew — the foot and Send's corner never move as a row comes or goes; the tag field keeps the plain shape.
Pictures dropped anywhere on the thread's pane are taken.
A quote of a sticker is its thumb alone (the reply row too), and a quote over a sticker reply keeps no overlap room.
Drafts per account and conversation on the device (web `lib/data/chatDrafts`, iOS `ChatDrafts`, Android `ChatDraftBook`): words, reply, note, card — never pictures; gone on send, when emptied, with the conversation and at sign-out; the list shows a draft after a pen, in `--terracotta-deep` (never the word 草稿).

## The thread, as drawn (from the design system)

A chat bubble is `lib/design/bubble` — the apps' recipe to the point (`seedFromId`, corners tucked in a run) — worn as a `clip-path` once measured, so what it carries (a link's picture, a shared card's cover, a sent picture — each bleeding 4 px past the box, where the wobble swings out) runs edge to edge and ends at its wobble, and a picture opens whole in its viewer; the thread's rows are memoized (`MessageRow`, actions from `ThreadActionsContext`) and its draft lives in `ThreadComposer`, so typing never draws the thread again.
The composer floats over the thread on the foot's paper — the page's cream behind it across the pane to the screen's edge, its top 12 a fade from the thread starting on the capsule's top edge, the solid part taking taps; in a pane the capsule doesn't fill (web ≥ 749, the apps' tablets and the Mac) the thread shows beside the capsule, sinking into a fade over the foot's last 28, a column of paper under the capsule from its middle — a panel open too (round 24: the fade ends where the panel begins, and the panel's paper and its top line run across the pane to the rule and the window's edge): one capsule — the field's wobble, no halo, its pen darkening (never terracotta) while written in, a ghost ＋ at its start, the text one line in exactly 48 growing to five (its padding from the real line height, so a larger text size never stretches it), Send as its end segment behind a wavy seam (tonal at rest, solid when there is something to send, never faded) — with the reply and what rides along inside it, over the text line (see Stickers and the composer); the thread runs on under it by its measured height (`useDockInset`) — only a scroll that moves the view up takes the reader off the newest message (`useThreadScroll`; a scroll the browser makes, its anchoring as the foot grows under a panel, never does), the way back down 12 over it, the letter's hint as the thread's last item; the apps draw the same.

### Holding a message, reactions

Holding a message on a touch screen — or tapping its reactions' chip — opens `MessageMenuOverlay` (round 16): the scrim (blurred 2 px, as a modal's) over the thread's body alone — from under its header's pen line, following the wave, on two panes from the rule beside the list, its leading edge the rule's own pen line (round 17: `threadBodyArea` reads the line, `rulePenLine`; no undimmed seam, and the header, the list and the rule stay crisp) —, the message lifted where it lies at 1.03, the five reactions floating directly above it on the scrim (`ReactionTray` `paper={false}`) and the actions directly below (`OrganicMenuPanel`, the menu sheet, the full time trailing at its end); when that doesn't fit, the message glides by the least distance inside the body (`lib/chat/menuPlacement`) — the menus never flip.
A dialog to the keyboard: the reactions a toolbar (← →), Tab on to the actions, Escape giving the focus back to what opened it.
Reactions sit in a chip in the gutter by the bubble's foot (`ReactionChips`), never under it, so no row's height changes for one; an unsent message is one dashed line (`UnsentBubble`).
With a mouse (hover + fine pointer) the bubble's ☺ opens only the tray on its paper sheet, 8 above it, and its ⋯ only the action list as a floating dropdown at the button (`MessagePopover`, `placePopover`), as wide as every dropdown (round 17 addendum: the 216–280 is a finger's) — no scrim, no lift.
The reactions are five hand-drawn stickers (`lib/design/reactions`, ids `heart · moved · laugh · hug · thumbs`, drawn by `atoms/ReactionGlyph` and the apps' generated `Reactions`: fixed colours as emoji keep, the rim the palette's `reaction-outline`, chalk at night); a client draws an id it doesn't know as nothing.

### The publish step's tags

The publish step's tags are one more of the composer's capsules (`TagField` on `segmentCapsuleShape`): 「標籤」 on its own line, the chosen tags under it as `TagPill`s in the 「AI 建議」 segment's tonal face and ink, no outline (round 17), then the field whose end segment says 「AI 建議」 (tonal) with nothing committed typed — an input method mid-word counts as nothing — and 「新增」 (solid) once there is; the field never moves under the caret when the pills wrap (the step scrolls by the difference), Backspace takes a tag back only from a hardware keyboard.
