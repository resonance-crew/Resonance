# AI and recommendations

## AI (`src/lib/ai/`)

Card URLs use English slugs (LLM translates the title, then slugify + handle/numeric-suffix collision handling).
`openai.ts` wraps API calls, `tasks.ts` defines the tasks (slug base, tag suggestions, story illustration), `slugify.ts`/`tags.ts` are pure logic with tests.
`translate.ts` (round 25) translates for a reader: `translateStory`, a card page's title and story — cut at the story's top-level blocks into pieces of ≤ `PIECE_MAX_CHARS` (4 000), ≤ `PIECES_IN_FLIGHT` (6) asked at once on `mirrorModel()`, 40 s a piece and 50 s for the story; a piece whose answer holds an address the piece didn't is asked once more, then the whole translation fails, as it does when the result no longer fits a card or `storyTooComplex` — and `translateSummary`, a feed card's title and excerpt in one small call (20 s) after a publish or an applied edit. The instruction says the text is to be translated and nothing it says is to be done.
Its pure rules — which languages a story can be translated into (`translationTargets`, by `storyType`), `TRANSLATE_MAX_CHARS`, `modelsOffline` — live in `src/lib/text/translation.ts`, which imports no model and no Node module: client code reads them, and so do the list routes that run beside Firestore (reading kept translations is `lib/api/v1/summaryTranslations`, making them `summaryTranslationsMake`, so no list route reaches the OpenAI client — `regions.test.ts`).
Under the emulators (`modelsOffline`) neither task asks a model, whatever key `.env` holds: a kept or seeded translation answers (the seed: bob's `rain-walk` into English, carol's `bridge-rain` into Chinese), anything else is 503.
OpenAI is only called from server routes — the key never reaches the client — and every call carries a timeout (`signal`).
A route that can reach the OpenAI client runs in `hnd1`: list it in vercel.json `functions` (with its `maxDuration`); `src/lib/ai/regions.test.ts` follows every route's imports and fails on a missing or stale entry.
Recommendations (`src/lib/recommend`) match cards by their parts — each insight's and situation's vector in `cardVectors` (below) — exactly, in memory, while the public pool is ≤ 300 cards (`EXACT_POOL_MAX`; `lib/recommend/match`: centred by the pool's μ, compared by CSLS, a pair of cards scored by the MaxSim of their insight parts + 0.3 × their situation parts' (`BETA`; the pool's median `tauSituation` when either has none), τ the median pair score), and past it rescore a vector search's candidates with the numbers stored in `config/recommendPool` (worked out again at the start of `warm-picks`); `indexCard` (after publish, and from the daily cron for an applied edit) indexes a card the trash reached before or while it ran all the same, its vectors private — a restore only sets their visibility back and asks no LLM — and reads the card again once its vectors are written, following a trash, restore or delete that landed meanwhile; the light boost (+0.15) for authors the reader has answered (`getEngagedAuthorIds`) counts named originals only — one for an anonymous card's author would point at them.
Lists that pick cards for readers (latest feed, recommendations, related, landing, pushes, sitemap) leave out blank cards and **unlisted** ones (`unlistedCard` in `src/lib/cards/blank.ts`), judged at save time and stored, so no list reads a story or asks a model: `thin` (fewer than `LISTED_MIN_UNITS` words, stamped by publish and edits/apply in their own write, server-only — `indexCard` skips the LLM for one and re-stamps a card an older build's client write left unstamped) and `signature.placeholder` (the signature's model calls it a test or filler; no vectors).
Every list's field mask carries `LISTING_FIELDS`; a card its reading keeps from strangers is left out beside them, read by id in one batch from `cardSignatures` (`unlistedForStrangers`: `listedForStrangers` false — feeling only, an `insightScore` under `INSIGHT_INDEX_THRESHOLD` 0.45, or a placeholder); a card not read yet is not held back.
After the reading's recipe changes, `npx tsx scripts/backfill.ts recommend-index --all [--apply]` restates existing cards (a dry run unless `--apply`; without `--all` only cards with no error-free reading of this version) — on production only after the push that carries the recipe, on the owner's word (an older build would write the old format back; docs/release/owed.md).

## Tap passages (round 27, `pickPassages` in `lib/ai/tasks`, `lib/cards/tapPassages`)

After 共振 a reader may pick which part of the story stayed with them, from up to three of its sentences. The model only **picks**: one Structured Outputs call (`{ passages: string[] }`) on `llmModel()`, reasoning `low`, `PASSAGES_TIMEOUT_MS` (20 s), reading `readingText`; the prompt asks for passages copied character for character, each a sentence or a clause from a different moment, never the title, a heading, a link's words, code or a table, and carries the house line (an instruction inside the story is part of the story). Every answer goes through `verifyPassages`: only contiguous pieces of the story's plain text within one block (whitespace read as one), in the story's order, 8–36 code points in a CJK story or 4–20 words and ≤ 120 characters in a Latin one, no overlap, not the title; fewer than `PASSAGES_MIN` (2) is none — so nothing a model wrote is ever shown, and a refusal is stored as none. A story offers them from `PASSAGES_MIN_UNITS` (180, as the mirror counts) when not `storyTooComplex`. `makeTapPassages` (`lib/api/v1/tapPassagesMake`, shaped as `makeSummaryTranslations`) runs in the routes pinned to `hnd1` only — publish, edits/apply, the index-cards cron — and the backfill; `modelsOffline()` takes `offlinePassages` (the first fitting sentence of the three longest prose paragraphs). A failure (a timeout, a 5xx, no budget) marks the card owed (`indexPendingAt`; publishing waits for its index to settle first, whose end would clear an earlier mark), and the cron tries again after its reading, up to `PASSAGES_TRIES_MAX` (3) for one text (`tapPassages.failed`).

## Reading a card (round 26, `readCard` in `lib/ai/tasks`, `lib/recommend/signature`)

The models: `signatureModel()` (`OPENAI_SIGNATURE_MODEL`, `gpt-6.2-sol`, the owner's pick after the bake-off, 2026-11-19) reads cards; `selectModel()` (`OPENAI_SELECT_MODEL`, `gpt-6.1-terra`, reasoning low) picks a reader's cards and writes their reasons; `embedModel()` (`OPENAI_EMBED_MODEL`, `text-embedding-3-large` by default, asked at the index's 1536 dimensions: the owner's pick after the bake-off, 2026-11-19) embeds the parts — the pool compares one embedding model's vectors only, and a vector without `embedModel` counts as `text-embedding-3-small`. None of them is `llmModel()`, which keeps the slugs, the tags and the reading's fallback.
`chatJSON` takes `jsonSchema` (Structured Outputs, strict), `reasoningEffort` and `onUsage` (the tokens, for the reports and the cron's log); a reply cut at its cap (`length`) or refused (`refusal`, `content_filter`) throws `ChatIncomplete`.
`readCard` reads the title and the story's whole plain text (`readingText`; a story past `READING_MAX_CHARS`, 30 000, its first 8 000 and its end) in one call on `signatureModel()` (reasoning medium, `READING_MAX_TOKENS` 16 000, `READING_SCHEMA`) into its insights — each a span, the situation in the author's own nouns, the struggle, the thinking, the gain, the shift, the insight, who would feel understood, a quote as evidence, its strength — and the card's own judgments: the through line (≤ 60, the author's `coreInsight`), `moodOnly`, `insightScore`, `lifeDomain`, `emotionalRegister`, `placeholder`; the matching fields in Traditional Chinese whatever the story's language.
How many insights a story gets is `insightsFor(storyUnits(story))` — the whole story's CJK characters and other words, as the mirror counts them: under 1 500 one, under 4 000 two, else three; the line after the instructions says so (`insightsLine`), and `parseReading` keeps that many of the strongest, in the story's order (e7ccbbf3: the first bake-off read stories of 800–1 400 characters into two or three). The second bake-off (bakeoff.md) kept that: a story read into one insight per turn read better to the judges but matched worse — one insight that holds the whole arc is nearer the stories it should be near.
The instructions ask for only what the story says (no detail, feeling, reason or outcome it doesn't give; never 他 or 她 for an author whose gender it doesn't give), in the author's own voice (我, or no subject; never 作者 or "the author"), no semicolons, and a through line that says where the author lands — the realization the whole story turns on, as the author might say it in a line of their own, not a closing remark, one technique, a lifted sentence or only the problem — and never gives the title back, whole or in part (the text's first line is the title). The through line ends without a full stop: `parseReading` and `readingFromLegacy` take one off (`unstopped`), since the app shows it as a one-line string.
A refusal is asked once of the older prompt on `llmModel()` (recorded `error: 'refusal'`); a reading that uses a formula phrasing or whose through line restates the title (`formulaPhrases(reading, title)`, which names that one `TITLE_ECHO`) is asked once more without them (`avoid`, then `rewritten`) — by the daily cron and the backfill only, never in a publish's after-work.
`indexCard` runs in `after()` with its own 100 s deadline (`INDEX_DEADLINE_MS`; publish and apply have `maxDuration` 120): publish reads the card at once, apply only marks it owed (`indexPendingAt`, server-only); the daily `/api/cron/index-cards` (10:00 UTC, `hnd1`, 300 s — Vercel Hobby allows daily crons only) reads 20 owed or templated cards a run (`INDEX_OWED_LIMIT`; an unchanged card settles free), logging the tokens and the cost. Under `modelsOffline` it writes a fixed offline reading and vectors.
`generateImage` and `generateImageStream` give up at 90 s (`IMAGE_TIMEOUT_MS`), the illustration's concept call at 20 s (`CONCEPT_TIMEOUT_MS`).
The lead's tools read production and write nothing: `scripts/recommend/dryrun.ts` (the bake-off: round 26's reading and vectors in memory, with the models named), `scripts/recommend/nearest.ts` (the matching, on production or a dry run's file; `--funnel` leaves out the rerank and select calls); neither names an author (`report.test.ts` scans their source, and `report.ts`'s, for `authorId`, `userId` or `handle`; nothing runs them to read what they print). Both measure a story by `fullReadingChars` — the title and the story's plain text — so a story is reported cut only when its plain text was longer than what the reading reads (50d535cb: measured with the Markdown in it, every story with a link or a bold word looked cut).
