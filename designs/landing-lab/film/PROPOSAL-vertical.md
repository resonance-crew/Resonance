# 雲海與天燈 · Clouds Below, Lanterns Above

**The idea in one line:** your scroll is the wind. It carries a story down from a shy writer's attic above the sea of clouds, through one whole day, to a listener by the sea. The answer rides a sky lantern back up through the night, and you reach the same window at the next sunrise.

## 0. The decision this angle forces

The owner's change is that the scene's layers slide up at different speeds and the next scene's layers rise from below. That is exactly what a **descending** camera sees. A card rising through a roof would show the opposite. So in this world **stories fall**, like letters and leaves. Every change on the way down is the owner's change, and the camera's own movement motivates it; it is not an effect laid on top.

The world turns the other way once, at the emotional turn, when the answer rises. Down is the day and up is the night. The film is a V, and so is its arc: letting go → nearly lost → found → answered → lifted home.

## 1. The world: one tall sheet of paper

| Top to bottom | Home of | Going down | Coming up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Summit: an attic on the peak's tip | 摺摺 | dawn | pre-dawn → sunrise |
| Sea of clouds | 點點 | morning | moonlit |
| Mountainside: cliffs, pines, a waterfall of paper strips, cranes | — | blue day | night, stars |
| Town on the slope: post office, bunting, rice terraces below | 牽牽 | golden | lit windows, lanterns |
| Shore: headland, lone tree, the sea | 聆聆 (悄悄 after dark) | dusk → evening | the turn |

- **Depth is the hour.** Light reaches the summit first and leaves it last, and night reaches the shore first.
- **Parallax:** the far sea and ranges hold the horizon while the near cliffs, clouds and roofs rush past.
- **Sun and moon:** the sun sits at infinite depth. It rises at the attic and sets into the sea; the moon crosses during the climb.
- **Two images need no explaining here:** the sea of clouds at sunrise, and sky lanterns (天燈) carrying written paper into the night.
- **The return costs almost no bytes.** The climb reuses the descent's plates in night colours, because the art pipeline's colours are live.

## 2. The script (~75 s at film speed)

**0 · 落筆 Writing (dawn, summit, 5 s; plays once on load).**
- The attic is cut away like a paper theatre. 摺摺 finishes its last lines under the lamp and holds the card to its chest; its folded corner stays shut, because it is shy.
- It lifts the card to the round window. The first ray of sun crosses the glass and the card **blooms into real card 1**. *Tap → its page.*
- **Page:** the film waits here.

**1 · 放手 Letting go (dawn, 5 s).** The card folds back into paper. 摺摺 hesitates, then lets go. The wind, which is the brand's three ribbons ≋, takes it out of the window, and it falls with a leaf's sway into the clouds. 點點 is bouncing on the clouds and leaps aboard. → *Change A: through the sea of clouds.*

**2 · 搭便車 The ride (blue day, mountainside, 7 s).** Out of the clouds' underside, 點點 rides the card like a sled, leaning to steer. Paper cranes part around it, the waterfall roars past, and pines whip by in front as the whole world opens below. *Tap 點點 → a handstand.* → *Change B: through the pines and the spray.*

**3 · 卡住了 The snag (golden, town, 6 s).**
- The card snags on paper bunting by the post office.
- 牽牽 is a tag on a parcel, and its 「給：」 has always been blank. It climbs the line and frees the card, and its own string winds around it.
- The card drops on, with 點點 on top and 牽牽 dangling below.
- *Tap 牽牽 → a mid-air bow.* → *Change C: down the rice terraces, gold turning rose.*

**4 · 墜落 The fall and the catch (dusk, shore, 7 s).** At the bottom of the world the wind dies. The card tumbles toward the darkening sea as the sun slides into it. Under the lone tree, 聆聆 is listening to the waves. It hears the paper, runs down the shore and catches the card a hand's breadth above the water. **Page.**

**5 · 讀與回 Reading and answering (dusk into evening, 10 s).**
- Card 1 blooms again in 聆聆's hands, larger and with its excerpt: this is the reader's moment.
- 聆聆 nods as it reads. Faint lines appear on its own body, because it writes what it hears on itself, and a paper heart swells.
- It takes a fresh card in its own lavender and writes back. **Real card 2 blooms** beside card 1.
- **Page.**

**6 · 點燈 Night falls (evening, 6 s).**
- A shadow on the rock stretches: 悄悄 wakes, and night spreads out of its paper. Pinhole stars come out and the town's windows light up above.
- 悄悄 lights a sky lantern and 聆聆 pins card 2 to it.
- 牽牽 ties the knot, and a tiny dog-eared square appears on its blank 「給：」. It is 摺摺's shape: now the tag knows who this is for.
- They all climb on, and up the slope the town lets its own lanterns go.
- → *Change D, the reversal.*

**7 · 升空 The climb (night, 8 s; plays itself on a forward nudge).** The lantern rises past the lit town and the waterfall, now silver, then up through the moonlit clouds. 悄悄 pricks stars into the sky as they go. Dozens of lanterns climb alongside, each carrying a real card, and the sky fills with stories. *Tap a lantern → that story.* The film holds while a lantern is hovered or focused.

**8 · 日出 The meeting (pre-dawn to sunrise, summit, 10 s).**
- 摺摺 is asleep at its desk; it waited all night. Lantern light wakes it and card 2 blooms.
- It reads, its heart swells, and for the first time it **lifts its folded corner**: the lines it keeps hidden show.
- 聆聆 steps onto the sill. They bow and stand side by side.
- 牽牽 ties the two cards together, and its string draws as ≋.
- The sun clears the clouds. 悄悄 curls up asleep on the sill, and the day comes back.
- **Page: the two real cards joined by ≋.**

**9 · 換你 Your turn (morning, 3 s).** 點點 pushes a blank card across the desk toward you and looks up. The hero line returns. End.

**What a visitor understands without reading:**
1. Someone writes (0).
2. They let it go to no one in particular (1–4); the blank 「給：」 says so.
3. Someone else reads it and answers with a card (5).
4. The answer finds its way back (6–7).
5. The two meet (8).
6. Yours is blank (9).

## 3. Scene changes

**Down: the owner's change, played by the world itself.** Each change passes through a **band**: the sea of clouds, then the pine canopy and spray, then the rice terraces.
- A thick near layer at 1.4–1.6× parallax sweeps up fastest, the mid layers slower, and the far ranges barely move.
- The next stratum rises from below in the same order, using the tech research's windows and seam lock, so there is always paper under paper.
- At the band's peak cover, the sky crossfades to the next hour behind it, so a palette swap never shows as a fade.
- The terraces are almost literally the owner's picture: strip after strip, each rising at its own speed.
- The card and its riders hold their place on screen, with a leaf's sway of ±6 %, while the world moves past. It is a match cut.

**Up (change D), only once.**
- It is the same math mirrored, and it comes right after a page.
- The lantern rises on screen 0.4 s before the camera follows.
- The climb plays itself.

So it reads as being lifted, not as rewinding.

## 4. Control: scroll is the wind, with buttons beside it

I use the tech research's hybrid:
- Changes settle, and acts finish at film speed after a forward move.
- Pages wait, and ▶ removes the waits. Any input during play returns to browsing.
- Keys: ←, →, K and Home. Each scene writes its `#scene` with `replaceState`.

**Why it fits this world:**
- **The gesture is the story.** On a phone your finger pushes the world up and the card falls past it, which is direct manipulation for the whole descent.
- **It gives the visitor a role.** You are the wind that carries stories between people; stop, and the story hangs in the air.
- **Native scroll keeps the page a page,** and the buttons serve anyone who would rather watch.

**The player** is one capsule at the bottom, shown after the prologue: ⏮ ▶ ⏭ · **altitude line** · 略過. On a phone it is ▶ · line · 略過.
- **The altitude line** is the progress bar drawn as the journey's profile: a pen line that dips from the summit to the shore and climbs back. The five heads are ticks where each one lives, and a paper card rides along it.
- It reads left to right like any slider (`role="slider"`, 「第 4 幕，共 9 幕：墜落」), and its shape tells you where in the world you are.

**The pair.** I use the data research's pair rule.

**Honesty and privacy:**

## 8. The five

- **摺摺, the writer at the top of the world.** Shy and alone, it lets a story go and waits all night. When it is answered it opens its corner: trust is the connection.
- **點點, the stowaway on the sea of clouds.** It rides the story down and is the visitor's companion and the comic relief. It is still looking for the rest of its story, and it ends by handing you the blank one.
- **牽牽, the tag at the post office.** It frees the stuck story, and its blank 「給：」 finds a name, as a shape rather than words. Its string becomes the ≋ between the two cards.
- **聆聆, the listener at the bottom of the world.** It hears the card, catches it, reads it, answers it, and rides up to meet the writer.
- **悄悄, the night itself.**
  - Its waking brings the dark, and its lantern starts the climb.
  - Its pin makes the stars, and its sleep lets the day back.
  - It appears only from evening, where its paper has enough contrast (art research §2).
