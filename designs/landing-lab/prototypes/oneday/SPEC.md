# SPEC C · 一天的戲 One Day — "told"   (id: oneday)

Base: SP/design/theatre.md, with Picturebook's beats (SP/design/picturebook.md — read its "heart that unfolds into a card" and the meeting at the gutter). Apply the critic's changes (SP/design/critique.md §6 C) and these lead decisions. Where this spec and the proposals disagree, this spec wins.

## The idea
The page is directed for the puppets from top to bottom, and it plays one day as you scroll — morning → noon → late afternoon → dusk → night. **No proscenium, drapes, valance, sun-and-hills scenery** (critic: kitsch). What stays from the theatre is its *one rule*: every section is content, then a **puppet lane** (one character tall), then a **floor** (the section's wavy pen line, which is also the next section's SectionEdge). Puppets stand only on floors and set pieces (card tops), never on words. Each wavy edge is a scene change: two or three paper flats (the existing `SectionEdge`, layered) slide past each other, scroll-scrubbed. The paper colour ramps through the day using palette tokens (cream → cream-dark → a gold mix → terracotta-light/terracotta → night), and dark mode stays coherent (an "evening show": every step a token).

4. **Late afternoon · 這裡沒有讚，只有回應 — the answer scene** (the centrepiece). A CSS **sticky stage** (native sticky, no pinning/wheel-jacking) with three beats scrolling past it (each beat ≤ 60 svh on the phone, ~70 svh on desktop; desktop stage 60 % left / beats 40 % right; phone stage sticks under the header at ~46 svh with the beats under it):
   ① 讀一個故事 — 「有人把自己的一段人生寫成了一張卡片」: card A on the stage; 聆聆 climbs onto it and **reads**.
   ② 不按讚，寫一張回應 — 「回應一個故事，用的是你自己的故事」: a hand-drawn **♥ appears and unfolds into card B** (a crossfade/morph of the heart's outline into a dashed card — MorphSVG only if the crossfade disappoints), 聆聆 climbs down and **writes** beside it; B's scribble lines draw in with its pencil.
   ③ 兩張卡片，從此相連 — 「公開署名的回應會讓作者知道是你，你們就能開始聊聊」: B slides next to A, the three waves ≋ draw between them, and **the two puppets meet at the gutter between the cards** — the note hand-over (`meet('note')` / a directed visit + meet), then 摺摺 **reveals** its folded corner.
   Cues: an IntersectionObserver per beat sets `data-beat` on the stage and cues the stage (`visit({ledge, slug, acts})`, `meet('note')`); a beat must stay in view 400 ms before firing; scrolling back replays the earlier cue. **Scroll scrubs only paper** (the lines, waves). Server HTML: the beats' text + a **three-panel comic** (each beat's stills on a small floor) that JS off, reduced motion and short screens (< 520 px tall, landscape phones) get — `@media (scripting: enabled) and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) and (min-height: 520px)` switches to the sticky stage from first paint so nothing shifts.

## Tech
CSS first: scroll-driven animations for the flats' slide at each edge, the paper ramp, stars appearing, the drawn lines; native sticky; scroll-snap. The engine per scene (`mountPalStrip`-like stages with ledges from the section's own geometry; generalise in your folder as `mountScene`), at most two scenes live. A ~3 KB cue controller. **GSAP is allowed** where CSS falls short (e.g. ScrollTrigger to drive the beats robustly, DrawSVG for the waves) — dynamically imported after idle; say what you used and why. No ScrollSmoother, no snap-jacking.
