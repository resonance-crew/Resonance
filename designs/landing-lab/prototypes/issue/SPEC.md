# SPEC B · 對頁 Facing Pages — "read it"   (id: issue)

Base: SP/design/editorial.md. Apply the critic's changes (SP/design/critique.md §6 B) and these lead decisions. Where this spec and the proposal disagree, this spec wins.

## The idea
Today's issue of a small literary magazine. Real stories set as words in big Ming type; a story printed facing the story that answered it; the 小紙團 as the issue's staff, working in the room the grid leaves for them — above all **「第四個字」**: the vertical title's first column 用故事 is one cell short of 回應故事, and a puppet stands in that empty fourth cell as the missing character; when it hops, a wave of ink weight ripples through the title.

## Sections
0. **Header** `SiteHeader`; **running head** under it (desktop/tablet only; none on the phone): 13 px small caps between two INK_LIGHT wavy hairlines — 「共振 RESONANCE　本期六篇新故事」 (NO date) and at its end the pause toggle 「讓小紙團休息」. Grid: `[margin][text][margin]`, margin = 1.5 × `--pal-unit` (180/150/0), folios 01 02 … in Playfair italic in the margin.
1. **封面 Cover** — zh: the **vertical h1** (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) in two columns 「用故事」｜「回應故事」 read right to left, in real cells (`--cover-cell` 168/140/92 px), 回應 in terracotta with the pen wave down its LEFT side; the empty 4th cell of the first column holds **摺摺** (still in HTML, live via a one-spot stage: `read`, `look`, a `wave` on first scroll, `reveal` on the 3rd tap; 點點 occasionally hops into the cell instead). Kicker 「真實的人生故事 ─」 (text + pen rule, no pill). Dek 「讀到打動你的故事，不必按讚——寫一張自己的故事回應它，兩個故事從此相連」. Cover line (desktop/tablet): 「本期封面」 + the newest card's first sentence in Ming + byline/不署名, title, minutes, 讀這篇. `GetTheApp`. One sage `DriftingBlobs` blob behind the cell (its spotlight). **The ripple**: once idle (≥ 1 s after load) a wave of weight runs through the seven characters (700 → 900 → 700, 480 ms each, 70 ms stagger, 0.03 em sideways nudge; 回應 peaks with scale 1.04) — `font-weight` on per-character spans works because Noto Serif TC is variable here (check `src/styles/fonts.ts`; if it is not variable, use transform-only ripple and say so); it runs again on the puppet's `hop` (stage event), and on tap/click of the h1, at most every 1.5 s. Server renders `<span class="sr-only">用故事回應故事</span>` + `aria-hidden` per-character spans. **en**: horizontal h1 「Answer stories / with stories」 left-aligned, line 2 indented one cell with 摺摺 in the indent; ripple = per-word baseline wave (transform only). At 200 % zoom / narrow container the zh title may switch to horizontal (`@container`). Phone: the title block at the right top, kicker at the left, dek and badges under it within the first 640 px.
2. **目次 Contents** (folio 02, h2 「本期目次」, dek 「六篇新故事，來自 Starry、Neo、念誠，和不署名的作者」 — built from the real bylines, **naming named authors and never counting anonymous ones**; if none is anonymous, drop that clause). Each row = a link to the card: big folio numeral in the margin; title in Ming 600 (34/28/22); pull quote (first sentence of the story, ≤ 36 graphemes CJK / ≤ 110 chars Latin, from `src/lib/markdown/sharePassages` `passageBlocks` or plain text) in Hei muted with hanging 「」; byline/不署名 · minutes · ≤ 2 TagPills; **a tiny cover thumbnail (hand-drawn clip) only where the card has a cover**. Rows separated by hand-drawn wavy rules that draw in on scroll (CSS view timeline on stroke-dashoffset). The h2 inks in (weight 200→700, opacity .15→1 per character, scroll-linked). Desktop with a mouse: the **sidenote** column (360 px) cross-fades to the hovered/focused row's cover cut-out + 4-line quote + tags. 點點 sits on the sidenote's top edge (desktop) and hops/looks when the choice changes (debounced 2 s); on the phone it sits in a ≋ dinkus row before the section. 翻閱所有故事 (tonal) → /home.
3. **對頁 Facing Pages** (folio 03, h2 「一個故事，和回應它的故事」, dek 「在這裡，喜歡一個故事的方式，是寫一個故事回它」). **Fake the pair from the six** (a comment says so: the real version reads the newest resonance's original, the owner's call): left page 「故事」 + title (Ming 40) + opening (Ming 26/1.75, ≤ 90 graphemes, ending 「……」) + byline + 讀全文; right page 「回應」 + 「回應〈A 的標題〉」 + the same. The gutter (1.6 u) is a vertical pen line with the brand's three waves crossing it, drawing scroll-linked. At the foot rule (the puppets' floor): **牽牽** and **點點** stills facing each other across the gutter; live, when the spread is 60 % in view, a meet strip runs `meet('note')`: they close in, `resonate`, 牽牽 hands 點點 the folded note; on the stage's `note` event page B's title ripples. Once per visit. **Desktop-only tactile graft**: page B can be dragged a little away (≤ 60 px, Draggable or pointer events), the gutter waves stretch with it, and on release it hops back in stop-motion steps. Phone: pages stack, the fold turns sideways (a horizontal wavy rule with the waves), the meet strip full-width between them (unit 80).
4. **最後一頁 The blank page** (folio 04, kicker 最後一頁, h2 「這一頁，等你來寫」, line 「不必寫得很好，只要是真的——你的故事，也許正好回應了某個人」, solid 寫下你的故事 → /write, tonal 先讀讀大家的故事 → /home, under them small badges). Same spread geometry: left = the newest story's opening ending 「……」 + 讀完它; right = a blank ruled page (rules hand-drawn at the story leading, INK_LIGHT `--soft-pen`), h2 on the first rule, buttons on the last. **聆聆** stands at the right end of the first rule with its pencil (`write`, then `point` at the button), its line 「嗯，我在聽」 in pencil grey Ming light. Rules draw in on view.
5. **本期編輯部 Colophon** — five slots, each a `PaperPalStill` (0.6 u) + name + role + line: 摺摺「封面」, 點點「找稿」, 牽牽「傳信」, 聆聆「旁註」, 悄悄「夜班」; link 「認識小紙團」 → /paper-pals. Static stills (hello on hover/tap if cheap).
6. **封底 Back cover** — the footer as a **night** band (not today's stock dark footer): wavy top, pinprick stars, 悄悄 napping by the logo, tagline 用故事回應故事, badges + trademark line, links, ©, pause toggle.

## Warmth (critic): a little colour back
Tiny covers in the contents, one `DriftingBlobs` per section where there is room (cover, spread), the night back cover, and the pastel puppets themselves. Ink on cream otherwise.

## Tech
CSS scroll-driven animations (`@supports (animation-timeline: view())`, `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`) for rules, h2 ink-in, gutter waves. **Motion** (motion.dev, vanilla `animate`/`stagger`/`inView`, dynamic import) for the ripple and the sidenote cross-fade. Pointer drag for page B (GSAP Draggable only if it is simpler). The engine via one small stage per section, a director keeping one live section at a time. No GSAP ScrollTrigger, no pinning.
