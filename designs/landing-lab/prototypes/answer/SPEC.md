# SPEC A · 換你說 Your Turn — "do it"   (id: answer)

Base: SP/design/answer.md. Apply the critic's changes (SP/design/critique.md §6 A) and these lead decisions. Where this spec and the proposal disagree, this spec wins.

## The idea
The visitor *does* the verb in the first screen: a real story on the left page, a blank card of their own on the right; tap a starter (or type one line), press 回應, and watch the line become a card linked to the story by the pen thread while 聆聆 folds it and 牽牽 carries it over. Nothing is stored or sent. Then: finish it for real (/write), or get the app.

## Sections (top to bottom)
1. **Header**: today's `SiteHeader`.
2. **The desk (hero)** — left-aligned h1 (96–112 px at 1440, 回應 accented; its pen wave runs on a little long, visibly becoming the thread), subtitle 「在這裡，你不按讚——讀完一個故事，就用自己的故事回應它」, the store badges (+QR on hover+mouse) **on every width, phones included** (on the phone in a row under the subtitle, before the desk). Then the spread:
   - **Left page, "有人寫下"**: one featured real card from the six — **no cover image** (title in Ming over 5–7 lines of its opening in the reading type, byline/匿名, minutes, 讀完整個故事 → its card page). Pick: listed, ≥ 120 chars of plain text, prefer a named author; fallback the newest. 「換一個故事 ↻」 cycles the six client-side (Flip or a 12 fps paper slide).
   - **Right page, "換你說"**: a **blank card** (`HandDrawnDashedBorder` if it exists, else your own dashed wobRect), NOT the chat composer: one title-size Ming line as the prompt 「這個故事讓你想起什麼？」, three starter chips (「我也曾經……」「這讓我想起一個人……」「讀完，我想說……」) which are the main path on a phone (tap chip → its words land on the card's first line → 回應), a plain borderless line field ≥ 16 px (iOS zoom) set in the story type, and a solid 回應 fill. The honesty line 「只是試試看，不會發布、也不會送出」 sits above the button.
   - **Idle hint** (Picturebook's ♥ → card): while the blank card is untouched and in view, a small hand-drawn ♥ outline sits on it and, every ~6 s, unfolds into a tiny card shape and back (stop-motion steps) — "a heart that refuses to be a heart". Stops once the visitor interacts; stops under the pause control.
   - Puppets: **聆聆** sits on the right end of the blank card's top edge (still in HTML; live: `read`/`look`, nods while typing — fake the nod with a `look` act or by a tiny CSS bob on the live layer if no `listen` act exists); **牽牽** stands at the gutter end of the thread (still in HTML; live: `beckon` once after ~6 s idle in view).
   - Desktop: two pages ~520 px with a 140–160 px gutter, tilted ±0.6°, spread top at ~y 430 so both puppets fit in the 900 px fold. Phone: stacked — story page, a short stretch of thread in the left margin, the blank card with both puppets on its top edge near the first screen's foot.
3. **The answer (result state)** — on 回應: your line Flips into the card's title position; the dashed outline redraws as a solid pen line (DrawSVG) and the card takes a paper colour (BrushWash-like spread); the thread across the gutter draws from your card to the story (DrawSVG); three captions (`role="status"`) arrive: 「你的卡片和它連在一起了」 · 「真的寫的時候，作者會收到你的回應」 · 「回應被接住，你們就連上了，可以開始聊」; the actions appear at once: **寫完這張卡** (solid → `/write`; the real seed hand-off is out of scope — link only) · the badges 「在 App 裡繼續」 · quiet 「再回應一個」 (resets with the next story). Puppets: a `meet('note')`-style hand-over 聆聆 → 牽牽 if you can direct it with the existing engine (`visit({ledge, slug, acts, meet:true})` / `meet('note')`), then 牽牽 crosses the gutter: **fake the tightrope** by hiding the live 牽牽 and moving a `PaperPalStill` along the thread with GSAP MotionPath in 12 fps steps with a 3 px bob, landing on the story card's top-left corner. If directing the duet proves unreliable in the time you have, fake the whole hand-over with stills + GSAP and say so in the report. The page never waits on a puppet (cap every wait at 1.5 s). Focus moves to the result card's title; reduced motion → the finished state in a 150 ms fade.
4. **The stories** 「還有人在等一個回應」 / subtitle 「真實的人、真實的經歷，每一張都可以回應」: the other five real cards. **Desktop: a two-row, bottom-aligned shelf** (cards standing on a drawn pen shelf line; no drag carousel). **Phone: a native scroll-snap row** (one card + 40 px of the next). **點點** hops along the shelf line / the row's top rule (its own small stage; still in HTML), `look`s down at a card. Each card has a small tonal 「回應這個」 that moves that story into the desk (scroll up + swap) — optional, first to cut. 瀏覽所有故事 → /home.
5. **The ending, at night** (replaces the terracotta band and the stock footer): one night band (`--band-night`-like token; check tokens.css / PaperPalsPage for the night band's tokens) — title 「每個人都有幾行，一直折著的字」, line 「準備好了再掀開——寫下來，也許正好回應了某個人」 (if the visitor answered above: 「你剛剛寫了「…」——把它寫完」 and the first button becomes 寫完這張卡), buttons 寫下你的故事 (solid) / 先讀讀大家的故事 (tonal), a big folded paper corner at the band's top-right that lifts ~30° as the band crosses mid-view (CSS scroll-driven, static half-lift fallback) while **摺摺** standing on the band's wave does `reveal` at that moment; then the footer merged into the same night: ≋ Resonance, tagline 用故事回應故事, 「晚安，再讀一個就好 →」 (/home), badges + trademark line, links, ©, pinprick stars, **悄悄** napping on the wave (twinkles on arrival). The pause control lives at the desk's foot and in the footer.

## Tech
GSAP core + Flip + DrawSVGPlugin + MotionPathPlugin for the send timeline, dynamically imported on first focus/pointerdown in the blank card or 2 s idle. CSS scroll-driven animations for the thread stretches drawing, the corner lift. The engine for live puppets (small stages per section, pattern from PalStrip/titleScene). No ScrollTrigger, no pinning.
